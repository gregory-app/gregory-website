01How it works

Connect. See. Ask.

No finance team needed to start, and nothing to rebuild when you hire one.

Connect

Shopify for sales, QuickBooks or Xero for the books, and the spreadsheet you plan in, in OneDrive or Google Drive. Gregory reads one report a month from each. It never posts to your books.

See

Each month’s actuals land in your forecast under a rule signed once. The app shows where you stand against plan, and what moved since last month.

Ask

Why did margin drop? Which costs grew fastest? Ask in plain words. The answer points at the report or the cell it came from, and changes nothing until you sign.
